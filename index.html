Google Filament Engine Architecture

PHYSICS ENGINE

Does Google Filament have physics?

No. Filament is strictly a real-time physically based rendering (PBR) engine designed for Android, iOS, Windows, Linux, macOS, and WebAssembly. It deliberately excludes physics to remain hyper-lean and embeddable.

To add physics to a Filament-based engine, games pair it with Bullet Physics, Jolt Physics, PhysX, or Rapier (Wasm).

NETWORKING

Does Google Filament have networking?

No. Filament contains zero networking code. Multiplayer is handled separately using WebRTC, WebTransport, or native sockets.

Google Filament HZB Architecture Specification

CHEAP & ZERO BANDWIDTH

Why is HZB (Hierarchical Z-Buffer) so efficient?

1. Single-Pass 2x2 Min/Max Downsampling: HZB generates a 5-level mip pyramid directly on the tile cache in a fraction of a millisecond (<0.06 ms).

2. Conservative Occlusion Culling: Before dispatching expensive fragment shaders on occluded objects (such as pillars behind walls), the bounding box is tested against the lowest fitting HZB mip. If the box is behind the HZB depth value, the entire draw call is rejected immediately.

3. Hi-Z Empty Space Skipping: Screen-space reflections jump multiple pixels per step by querying higher mip levels when raymarching empty air, reducing ray samples from 64 down to 8!

Physically Based Bloom Pipeline

HDR ENERGY CONSERVING

Multi-Scale Progressive Blur Architecture

1. Luminance Curve Extraction: Pixels with radiance above the knee threshold are preserved with soft quadratic roll-off, isolating neon cores, gems, and specular highlights.

2. Pyramid Downsampling & Upsampling: Progressive 13-tap tent filter downsamples across 4 FBO mip targets, followed by 3x3 tent upsampling with 0.85 radius weighting to eliminate temporal flicker.

3. Spectral Chromatic Fringe: Blue and red color channels are slightly offset during composite to replicate camera lens aperture refraction and anamorphic glare.

Crepuscular Scattering Architecture

RAYMARCHED SHAFTS

Screen-Space Radial Raymarching

1. Light Position Projection: The main directional sun light is projected into screen-space UV coordinates with frustum clipping handling.

2. Shadow Raymarching: For each pixel, the shader marches along the vector pointing toward the screen-space light source, sampling scene occluders (walls, columns, catwalks).

3. Mie Phase Forward Scattering: Atmospheric aerosol scattering accumulates illuminated radiance with exponential distance decay, producing authentic crepuscular volumetric god rays streaming past obstacles.
